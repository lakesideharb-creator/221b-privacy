221B Casebook — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

221B Casebook is an offline card deduction game. We respect your privacy.

Data collection

The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. We do not require an account, and we do not operate servers that receive your information.

Tracking & analytics

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking tools.

Game progress

Your progress and settings are saved only on your device and are never uploaded anywhere.

Purchases

If the app offers in-app purchases, they are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment information.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from any user.

Changes

Any changes to this policy will be posted on this page.
